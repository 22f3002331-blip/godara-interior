About Godara Interior

Project‑driven interior and civil works execution company.

Godara Interior handles complete interior and civil work execution for corporate offices, IT parks and commercial facilities. The company takes up work packages such as flooring, wall finishes, wet‑area tiling and civil modifications as per client drawings and technical specifications.

With experience across multiple campuses and facilities, Godara Interior works closely with project managers, architects and contractors to deliver consistent finish quality and coordinated handover of interior spaces.

  • Company: Godara Interior
  • Location: Hyderabad, Telangana
  • Focus: Interior & civil execution for corporate and commercial spaces
  • Work style: Drawings‑based, schedule‑driven project delivery

Our Services

Interior and civil work packages executed on site.

Floor & Wall Tiling

Installation of vitrified, ceramic and specialty tiles for office floors, corridors, lobbies and wet areas with proper levels and joints.

Stone & Marble Works

Marble, granite and stone fixing for reception zones, staircases, lift lobbies and special highlight areas.

Civil Modifications & Block Work

Block work, partitions and basic civil alterations such as openings, wall changes and plinth adjustments as per design.

Wet‑Area & Toilet Works

Complete tiling and related civil works for toilets, pantries and service zones including slopes, skirting and dado heights.

Finishing & Handover Support

Final touch‑ups, minor repairs and coordinated handover of work fronts with project and facility teams.
